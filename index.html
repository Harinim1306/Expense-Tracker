BOB Partner Pairing & Reimbursement Reminders

Pair with your partner so when they spend on BOB, you get notified with reminders until you mark "Paid".

HOW IT WORKS Dual BOB Settlement Workflow:
  1. Partner Spends on BOB: Choose "Partner Spent" when logging, or send your partner the request link to log it from their phone.
  2. Active Reminder: TrackFlow pins a reminder on your Dashboard: "You should send ₹[Amount] for [Purpose] to your partner". The money is NOT deducted yet.
  3. You Pay Partner: Tap "I Paid". The reminder stops immediately, and the amount is deducted from BOB account balance!

Mobile Screen & Offline Setup

100% Offline • Zero Laptop Server Dependency • No Login Required

1. How to run on phone without laptop running

TrackFlow is a standalone Progressive Web App. All data stays saved directly inside your mobile phone storage.

  • Free 1-Click Hosting: Host this folder on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel to get a private link (e.g. https://mytracker.vercel.app).
  • Local WiFi: Open on your phone browser using local IP (e.g. http://192.168.x.x:3000).
  • Direct File: You can also send the bundled standalone.html to your phone directly!

2. Add to Mobile Screen as App / Widget

Android (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu at top right.
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  3. A direct icon is added to your home screen!
  4. Long-press the icon for quick expense shortcuts!
iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow) at bottom.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add at top right.
  4. Opens full-screen like a native iPhone app!

3. Fully Offline

Once installed to your phone, Service Worker caches all code. You can switch on Airplane Mode or turn off your computer — the app and all transactions work seamlessly offline.
